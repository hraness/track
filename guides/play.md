Ctrl-P turns your computer keyboard into a piano for the focused
track, so you can play and record ideas by hand. Esc leaves it.
The keys are drawn as they sit on your keyboard, each with its note,
or on a kit its drum (kick on `a`, snare on `s`), lit while it sounds.

## Ask

- "record what I play over four bars, quantized to sixteenths"

## Type it yourself

- `play on` · `play off` · `play degrees` · `play in-key`
- `click 40%` · `count-in 2` bars · `grid 1/16` record quantize

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Project › click · count-in bars · grid

## Keys

- `a s d f g h j k l` white keys · `w e t y u o p` black keys
- `z` `x` octave · `c` `v` velocity · Shift + note sustains · Tab latch
- `r` record · `R` replace · Space play · `m` click · `q` chord mode
