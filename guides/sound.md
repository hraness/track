Every track plays one instrument. Sound shapes the focused track: its
instrument, preset, parameters and how its notes are played.

## Ask

- "make the lead a bright pluck"
- "a felt piano, softer"
- "give me a warm pad preset" · "find a gritty 808"

## Type it yourself

- `/presets` browses every preset: moving plays each one,
  enter keeps it, esc reverts
- `preset warm-pad` · `preset find fm bell` · `preset next`
- `instrument piano` · `synth preset pluck` · `synth lpf 800`
- `piano felt` · `bowed cello` · `wind flute` · `grain cloud`
- Tip: `try synth lpf 400` lets you hear a change on a loop first

## Menu

- Ctrl-K › Sound › preset library · instrument · preset
- Ctrl-K › Sound › synth filter · instruments

## Keys

- In a value row ←→ adjust · Enter opens its fader · `x` reset
- Presets: type to search · `→` similar · `*` star
- Play mode: `,` `.` previous and next preset
